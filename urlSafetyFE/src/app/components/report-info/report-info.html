Report Info

Google Safe Web Browsing API:
40% of the safety score.

The safety report check that is performed using the Google Safe Web Browsing API, sends the submitted url to google to be checked against a database of known unsafe urls.

SSL Certificate:
15% of the safety score.

The ssl certificate check, verifies if the submitted url is in possession of a SSL/TLS certificate. A ssl (Secure Socket Layer) certificate is used to ensure an encrypted connection between users and servers. A tls (Transport Layer Security) is a more updated and secure version of SSL, but security certificates are still refered to as SSL as it is a more common term.

SSL Expiration:
10% of the safety score.

The ssl expiration check, returns the number of days the ssl certification is valid for the submitted url.

  • A certificate with over 50 days of validity remaining is generally considered Healthy/safe.
  • A certificate with more than 30 days of validity remaining is generally considered suspicious/expiring soon.
  • A certificate with more than 0 days of validity remaining is generally considered to be very suspicious.
  • And a certificate with 0 days of validity remaining is considered expired/dangerous, as it is no longer encrypted.
Domain Age:
15% of the safety score

The domain age check, returns the number of days the the domian for the submitted url has existed.

  • A domian age of less than 30 days is considered unsafe.
  • A domian age of less than 180 days is considered risky.
  • A domian age of less than 360 days is considered suspicious.
  • And a domian age of more than 360 days is considered safe.
URL Analysis:
20% of the safety score

The url analysis checks, analyses the structure of the submitted url for anything suspicous. The url analysis checks for suspicious keywords and cloud domains stored in the url, some suspicious keywords include:

  • payment
  • free
  • login
  • prize
  • storage.googleapis.com
  • amazonaws.com

These keywords and domains are considered suspicious because they are often used in phishing or malicious websites.

The number of subdomians in the url and the depth of the url is also considered by counting the number of:

  • dots that appear in the url.
  • slashes that appear in the url.

A url with a high number of subdomains and url depth is consisdered suspicious as cybercriminals often use such url to hide malicious files and malware.
